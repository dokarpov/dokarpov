## Selected product work

### [Oído — service commerce for independent businesses](https://dokarpov.github.io/oido-site/)
A browser-first merchant proposition spanning multilingual storefronts, menu conversion, ordering examples, tips, feedback and pilot onboarding.

**Product focus:** merchant setup friction, customer journeys, pricing hypotheses and evidence-led pilot design.  
**Evidence boundary:** public browser beta and illustrated flows; production payment settlement, paying-merchant traction and GMV are not established.  
[See the product](https://dokarpov.github.io/oido-site/) · [Product scope and validation](https://github.com/dokarpov/oido-site#product-validation-priorities)

### [Intake Integrity — safer identity decisions before CRM writes](https://dokarpov.github.io/intake-integrity-site/preview/)
When two customer records plausibly match, guessing can corrupt downstream history. This research project explores holding ambiguous identities for human review instead of forcing a match.

**Product focus:** role separation, uncertainty, review decisions, downstream risk and operator workflows.  
**Evidence boundary:** a synthetic [guided scenario](https://dokarpov.github.io/intake-integrity-site/preview/) and a separately tested [engineering reference](https://github.com/dokarpov/intake-integrity-lab), not a production CRM integration.

### [Rilatu — traceable freelance work evidence](https://dokarpov.github.io/rilatu/)
An interactive research concept connecting an agreement, an invoice and a payment so that a reviewer can distinguish what the records support from what remains unverified.

**Product focus:** evidence provenance, decision context and understandable handoffs.  
**Evidence boundary:** browser-based [synthetic walkthrough](https://dokarpov.github.io/rilatu/#demo); no real verification or customer outcome is claimed.

## Decision and policy systems

Two small, testable reference implementations supporting the same principle from different directions: [Decision Systems](https://github.com/dokarpov/decision-systems) models **who decides next**; [Policy Kit](https://github.com/dokarpov/policy-kit) models **whether an action may execute**. They are engineering explorations, not separate claims of commercial adoption.

---

**How to read these projects:** an interactive demo, passing automated tests, a production integration and externally validated customer value are different kinds of evidence. I distinguish them rather than treating one as proof of another.
