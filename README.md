## Products

Products below have different development stages. **Interactive demos use example data unless stated otherwise; they are not live customer environments.**

### Oído — Digital storefronts for local businesses

Turns existing menus and service lists into multilingual, mobile-first storefronts accessible through QR codes and links. Explores ordering, service options, tips, feedback and merchant reporting without replacing existing POS systems.

**Role:** Founding CPO · product strategy, merchant and guest experiences, pilot design.  
**Available:** Interactive browser beta with sample merchants and customer journeys. Payments and bookings are not production-ready.

[Website & interactive demo](https://dokarpov.github.io/oido-site/) · [Investor overview](https://dokarpov.github.io/oido-site/investors/) · [Source](https://github.com/dokarpov/oido-site)

### RoleHint — Job search and application decisions

Finds vacancies, checks hiring constraints, maps job requirements to supported candidate experience and keeps application decisions and history together.

**Available:** Backend components and tests; illustrative browser workspace. The complete authenticated web experience is still in development.

[Product site](https://dokarpov.github.io/rolehint/site/) · [Try the sample workspace](https://dokarpov.github.io/rolehint/site/workspace.html) · [Source & implementation](https://github.com/dokarpov/rolehint)

### Intake Integrity — Safer enquiries before CRM entry

Checks incoming enquiries for identity ambiguity, preserves consent and evidence, and routes uncertain records for human review before downstream CRM delivery.

**Available:** Interactive scenario using fictional enquiries, alongside a separate engineering prototype of intake, review and delivery controls. No live customer CRM connection in the public demo.

[Try the review scenario](https://dokarpov.github.io/intake-integrity-site/preview/) · [Product site](https://dokarpov.github.io/intake-integrity-site/) · [Engineering prototype](https://github.com/dokarpov/intake-integrity-lab)

### Planriva — Architecture discrepancy review

Connects drawing and model references with potential discrepancies so architects can inspect evidence, review changes and retain their decisions.

**Available:** Interactive concept with sample project information, not a connected Archicad or BIM production workflow.

[Product site](https://dokarpov.github.io/planriva-site/) · [Try the review prototype](https://dokarpov.github.io/planriva-site/preview/) · [Source](https://github.com/dokarpov/planriva-site)

### Rilatu — Traceable proof of work and income

Organizes contracts, invoices and payments into an evidence trail for situations where professional or financial history needs to be explained and checked.

**Available:** Interactive demonstration with fictional records; no actual document verification.

[Website & prototype](https://dokarpov.github.io/rilatu/) · [Source](https://github.com/dokarpov/rilatu)

### DARIA.EXE — Decision-making game

A browser game about investigating incomplete information, choosing actions under uncertainty and seeing their consequences.

**Available:** Playable browser release with Quick Play and Investigation modes.

[Play the game](https://dokarpov.github.io/daria-exe/) · [Source](https://github.com/dokarpov/daria-exe)
